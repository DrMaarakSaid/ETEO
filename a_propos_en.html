📚 About ETEO

E-learning To Every One – Excellence in Dental Education

🌍 ETEO – A global platform, with a Moroccan base

The majority of clinical and scientific knowledge shared is universal (anatomy, pathology, therapeutics, materials, radiology, etc.).

However, certain ETEO modules – particularly those related to legal, fiscal, social, regulatory and administrative aspects – may vary from one country to another due to local legislation, healthcare systems, and professional regulations.

  • 🔹 By default, ETEO is based on the MOROCCAN framework (Moroccan Public Health Code, ONCD Code of Ethics, AMO, CNSS, DGI, Moroccan Labor Code, etc.) for these specific modules.
  • 🔹 Local adaptations are currently being deployed for other countries (France, Belgium, Switzerland, Canada, Tunisia, Algeria, Senegal, Ivory Coast, Cameroon, United States, etc.).
  • 🔹 Practitioners from other countries are encouraged to refer to their national legislation and to contact us to contribute to local adaptation.
